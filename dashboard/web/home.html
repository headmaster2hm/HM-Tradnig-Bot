THE SIGNAL STACK
Three indicators. One clean signal.
Layered the MetaTrader way: RSI(14) is plotted first, then a fast EMA(48) is drawn on top of it, and a slow EMA(50) on top of that. Crossovers decide direction.
01
RSI(14)
Momentum gauge. Distance from the 50 midline and its slope power the 0–100 confidence score.
02
EMA(48) fast
A quick average drawn over the RSI line to catch turns early and filter noise.
03
EMA(50) slow
The confirming trend line. Crossovers of EMA(48) and EMA(50) fire the BUY / SELL signal.
