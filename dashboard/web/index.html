How the signal works

RSI layered the MetaTrader way

The strategy layers three indicators the MetaTrader way: RSI(14) is plotted first, then EMA(48) is drawn on top of RSI, then EMA(50) on top of EMA(48). Crossovers between the two EMAs decide the trade direction.

01
Open a BUY

EMA48 crosses above EMA50 and RSI > 50, with no BUY already open.

02
Open a SELL

EMA48 crosses below EMA50 and RSI < 50, with no SELL already open.

03
